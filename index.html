    Amplitude spectrum

    Dashed lines: spindle frequency N/60 and harmonics. Red line: mounted natural frequency. Shaded: above Nyquist (fs/2). Turning forces are mostly steady, so the spectrum is small unless runout or an interrupted cut is switched on.
